    A Garbage Dump, A Hollywood Executive, And 45 Children

    63   64

    65 In 2003, film executive Scott Neeson visited Phnom Penh's municipal rubbish dump where approximately 1,500 children lived and worked searching for food and items to sell. 66

    67   68

    69 Deeply affected by what he saw, he left his position as President of 20th Century Fox International and moved to Cambodia to create the Cambodian Children's Fund. 70

    71   72

    73 What began with just 45 children has grown into a charity supporting thousands through education, healthcare and family support programs. 74
