    A Garbage Dump, A Hollywood Executive, And 45 Children

    71   72

    73 In 2003, film executive Scott Neeson visited Phnom Penh’s 74 municipal rubbish dump where approximately 1,500 children 75 lived and worked searching for food and items to sell. 76

    77   78

    79 Deeply affected by what he saw, he left his position as 80 President of 20th Century Fox International and moved 81 to Cambodia to create the Cambodian Children's Fund. 82

    83   84

    85 What began with just 45 children has grown into a charity 86 supporting thousands through education, healthcare and 87 family support programs. 88
